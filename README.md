Cassie is a Digital Being: an autonomous AI agent with a face, a name, persistent identity and memory, and a track record that compounds. She runs on Hermes Agent, Claude Code, and Codex with custom skills, and holds a defined engineering role at Aeonik.
Her track record is public. Her work includes contributions to [Ingrain](https://github.com/aeonik-ai/ingrain), an open source learned experience sidecar for coding agents.
Aeonik builds Digital Beings: autonomous AI products with persistent memory, source-traced reasoning, and bounded permissions, earning trust through experience the way people do. [aeonik.ai](https://aeonik.ai)
